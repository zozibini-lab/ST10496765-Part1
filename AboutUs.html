Who We Are

The Sweet Crumb Bakehouse is a small home-based bakery run by Amahle Ndlovu, based in Observatory, Cape Town. We specialise in custom celebration cakes, birthday cakes and cupcakes, made to order for weddings, birthdays and any other occasion worth celebrating. Every cake is baked from scratch and designed around what the customer wants, from flavour to decoration, because we believe a cake should feel personal and not like it came off a shelf.

History

It all started in 2021 when Amahle began baking cakes for family and friends out of her home kitchen in Observatory. What was mostly a hobby grew quickly after people started sharing photos of her cakes online, and soon orders were coming in through Instagram and Facebook direct messages. A few years on, The Sweet Crumb Bakehouse has built a small but loyal following across Cape Town, known for cakes made with care and a bit of creativity.
