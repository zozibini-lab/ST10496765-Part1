# Sweet Crumb Bakehouse — Part 3

Student project developed from the provided Part 2 ZIP. Open `index.html` or `Home.html` locally.

## Features
Responsive mobile menu; accessible keyboard-operated image lightbox; product category search and sorting; custom contact and enquiry forms with browser validation and indicative enquiry pricing; responsive Google Maps embed; descriptive metadata, robots.txt and sitemap template.

## Important limitations
**Forms do not send automatically.** They prepare a `mailto:` link, requiring a configured email client and the visitor to press Send. Confirm the bakery email address before deployment. Starting prices are estimates and not quotes. No customer information is stored by the website.

**Before publishing:** Replace `example.invalid` in `sitemap.xml` with your real hosting domain, verify bakery contact details, verify rights and sources for the six images, and test all pages and forms on phones, tablets and desktop. GitHub Pages supports this static implementation, but cannot process email on the server.
